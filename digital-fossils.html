THE IDEA BEHIND THE WORK

Memory,
set in stone.

Digital Fossils is the second chapter of Arctavis’s digital chronicle on Litecoin. Crystals, fossilized blocks, and mineral relics turn the architecture of blockchain into an imagined archaeological record.

Mid-poly crystal architecture gives shape to proof of work, privacy, scarcity, and the memory of the chain. Fifteen artifacts, numbered 016–030, preserve these ideas as digital fossils.

Conceived and directed by Arctavis. Created in collaboration with AI.
