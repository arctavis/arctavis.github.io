THE IDEA BEHIND THE WORK

History, built
from possibility.

Historical Genesis is the first chapter of a digital chronicle inscribed on the Litecoin blockchain. Across fifteen works, a cryptographic republic takes shape: through independent miners, debates over scalability, privacy, and a new cultural frontier.

American monumentalism meets science-fiction minimalism and mid-poly geometry. Here, blockchain becomes architecture, law, labor, energy, and collective memory.

Conceived and directed by Arctavis. Created in collaboration with AI.
